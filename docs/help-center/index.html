Data you can clone

Learn how Clonix duplicates your entire Shopify store catalog, themes, and settings with Zero-404 link rewriting and zero spreadsheet dependency.

What you can clone:

  • Products and Collections: All titles, HTML descriptions, tags, options, variant pricing, barcode/SKUs, and collection rules.
  • Metafields and Metaobjects: Custom schema definitions and entries (with topo-sorted bi-directional references).
  • Navigation and Redirects: Multi-level nested menus and 301 URL redirects.
  • Published Themes & Assets: Complete liquid code, sections, theme settings, and CDN asset images.
  • Pages, Blogs, and Articles: Rich formatted content with automatic internal link remapping.
  • Files & Media: Direct streaming via Cloudflare R2 vaults with zero file-size corruption.
Zero Spreadsheet Guarantee: Unlike Matrixify where you must manage confusing Excel formulas and MERGE/REPLACE command columns, Clonix operates via a direct 1-click cryptographic handshake.

How it works

Clonix operates on a 14-Stage Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) Scheduler powered by pg-boss and PostgreSQL. When you connect two stores via their unique 8-character pairing keys:

  1. Stage 0 (Preflight Probe): Reads the source store catalog in read-only mode to extract products, custom liquid templates (e.g. newmedda, newqabz), and generates an audit report.
  2. Zero-Leak R2 Staging: Streams bulk GraphQL JSONL data directly to encrypted Cloudflare R2 buckets, bypassing memory limits and eliminating Duplify's 3,000 product trap.
  3. Atomic Stage Barrier: Advances through stages (Locations, Files, Definitions, Products, Menus, Theme, and Redirects) with automatic retry backoff.
  4. Stage 13 (Zero-404 Storefront Verifier): Crawls the destination store to verify that all image links and product handles resolve with HTTP 200 and zero 404s.
